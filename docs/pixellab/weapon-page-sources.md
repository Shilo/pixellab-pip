# Weapon Page Sources

The runtime weapon, fighting and armour pages (`skills/pixellab-pip/references/`) are written in our own words.
These are the public works and reference sheets each page was described from. They are kept here because they add no
agent decision at runtime.

## weapon-axe.md

Fiore dei Liberi (pollaxe), Talhoffer 1467 (Mordaxt), P. H. Mair (halberd), Muybridge 1887 (hatchet and pick swings), a CC BY-SA axe-swing sprite, and four axe and halberd sketch sheets.

## weapon-bow.md

Hansard 1840, Hokusai's Manga 1817, Morse 1885, the Longman & Walrond 1894 and Saxton Pope 1923 photographs, two CC0 archer sprite sets, and several archery diagram and action-pose sketch sheets.

## weapon-dagger.md

Fiore dei Liberi, Talhoffer 1467, Meyer 1570, US Army FM 21-150 and FM 3-25.150, and a CC0 knife sprite set.

## weapon-effects.md

three 8-direction top-down attack breakdowns, two character sword sheets (CC0, CC BY 3.0), and CC0 sheets of slash arcs, painted trails, dithered crescents, hit flashes, sparks, ray impacts, impact bursts, shockwave rings, earth impacts and an arrow trail.

## weapon-katana.md

Saito's Jikishinkage-ryu kata manual (1901), a 1938 school kendo manual, Rama's iaido drawings and chronophotograph, a kamae chart, three free samurai sprite sets, and two kamae and combo sketch sheets.

## weapon-mace.md

Talhoffer 1467 (club and tall shield, weighted veil), Paulus Hector Mair (war and threshing flail) and a CC BY-SA LPC mace slash sheet.

## weapon-spear.md

Jacob de Gheyn's pike drill, an anonymous Rijksmuseum pike sheet, US Army FM 23-25 (bayonet), Talhoffer 1467 (mounted spear), Muybridge plates 360/361 (javelin), an LPC spear sprite, and three spear and glaive sketch sheets.

## weapon-staff.md

Allanson-Winn & Phillipps-Wolley 1890, Riboni 1862, Hokusai Manga vol. 6, Meyer 1570, US Army FM 21-150 (short stick), a CC0 bo-attack sprite set, a CC BY 3.0 wizard sheet, and two acrobatic-staff sketch sheets.

## weapon-sword-and-shield.md

Royal Armouries Ms. I.33, Marozzo 1536 and 1568, Di Grassi 1570, Talhoffer 1467 (dueling shield), a CC0 knight sprite set, and a sword-and-shield gesture strip.

## weapon-sword.md

Fiore dei Liberi (Getty Ms. Ludwig XV 13), Talhoffer 1467, Meyer 1570, Royal Armouries Ms. I.33, a CC0 knight sprite set, and several guard, attack and keyframe sketch sheets.

## weapon-war-hammer.md

Muybridge 1887 (plates 374, 376, 377), Talhoffer 1459 (Thott) and 1467, and three maul and leap-smash sketch sheets.

## martial-arts.md

Muybridge 1887 (punches, sparring, a takedown), a 1900s boxing guide, US Army FM 21-150 (kicks and throws), a CC0 cartoon boxer sprite, and several punch, kick, throw and kata sketch sheets.

## armor-clothing.md

Viollet-le-Duc 1874 (plate joints, a 15th-century harness), Bashford Dean's 1921 Met handbook (a Gothic harness, gauntlets), Muybridge 1887 (a draped gown walking, a trained dress turning), three CC sprite sheets (an LPC plate walk, a cloaked archer, a caped knight), and many cape, cloak, turnaround and samurai armour sketch sheets.
